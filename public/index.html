Questions

Good to know

Do you deliver?

Yes. Free local delivery on $50+ orders around Caledon, Bolton, Brampton and Vaughan, and free delivery across the wider GTA — Toronto, Peel, York, Durham and Halton — on $150+. Orders under $50 are ready for pickup in Caledon or Bolton.

How long does an order take?

Most orders are ready within a week of approving your proof. Large or event orders need more time, so tell us your date when you ask for a quote.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum for personal orders. A single keychain is fine. Business pricing starts at 25 pieces.

What kind of photo should I send?

The original photo from your phone works best. Screenshots and photos saved from social media are often too small to print sharply. For logos, send the largest file you have.

Are mugs and tumblers dishwasher safe?

Hand-wash printed drinkware to keep the print looking new. We keep ink off rims and lips.

Can you print designs with characters or sports team logos?

We can only print designs you have the rights to use, such as your own photos, your business logo, or original artwork.
